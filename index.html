Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How does automatic fee tracking actually work?
Every member's plan has an expiry date. The moment that date passes, GymGurus AI moves the member out of Active and into Pending Fees automatically — no one has to check manually.
Do I need technical knowledge to set this up?
No. Most gym owners are fully set up within a day. Our team also offers a guided onboarding call as part of every plan.
Can I send announcements through my existing WhatsApp group?
Yes. GymGurus AI connects directly to your gym's WhatsApp group, so members keep receiving updates in the place they already check.
Is my member data secure?
Yes. All member data is encrypted and backed up continuously, and only you and your authorised staff can access it.
Can I switch plans later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time as your gym grows, with no lock-in contracts.
